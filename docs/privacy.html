Roam Privacy Policy

Applies to Roam for iPhone, version 1.0.
Last updated: 30 July 2026 · Effective from the public release of version 1.0.
简体中文 · Terms of Use

1. The short version

Roam 1.0 has no account, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking code of any kind. Your recorded tracks, your photos, your videos and your voice notes are written to storage on your own iPhone and are never uploaded to us. We operate no server that receives your rides, because there is nothing for you to sign in to.

The app reaches the network for one thing, and only one: map imagery to draw underneath your route. A tile request says which square of the world is on screen and carries no identity, no track and no media. Section 4 describes it in full, because it is the only thing about you that ever reaches an outside company.

The app also talks to Apple's App Store when you buy or restore Roam Pro. That exchange is handled by iOS, not by us: we never see your payment details, and we receive no identifier for you from it.

Roam's data directories are marked with iOS file protection, so their contents are encrypted at rest and are not readable before the first unlock after a restart. Downloaded map packs are deliberately excluded from your device backup, because they can be downloaded again and there is no reason to spend your iCloud storage on them. Your rides, your media and your thumbnails are included in your device backup, which is how a new iPhone gets your history — that backup is Apple's, governed by Apple's terms, and we cannot see inside it.

3. What Roam does not do

  • No account. There is nothing to register, nothing to log into, and no profile of you anywhere. Settings contains a row that says so.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting service. No Firebase, no Sentry, no Amplitude, no equivalent. The app contains no analytics SDK.
  • No advertising, and no tracking. The app does not read the advertising identifier, does not present a tracking request, and shares nothing with any data broker, ad network or measurement partner. Its App Store privacy manifest declares tracking as false, with no tracking domains.
  • No selling or renting of data. There is no data of yours in our hands to sell.
  • No social features, no leaderboard, no cloud sync. Nothing you record is visible to anybody else unless you export a file and send it yourself.

4. Every network connection the app can make

This is the complete list. If you put the phone in Airplane Mode, recording still works — satellite positioning needs no network — and everything except the map imagery behaves normally.

4.1 Apple Maps

The default basemap under your route is drawn by Apple's own map component built into iOS. Displaying it sends map requests to Apple. Those requests are handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy; Apple states that its Maps service is designed not to associate map requests with an Apple Account. Roam sends Apple no track of yours, no media and no identifier of ours.

4.2 Roam's own map tiles

The alternative basemap — the one drawn from our own tiles rather than Apple's — is served from storage we operate ourselves at Cloudflare R2, currently at pub-b18ac9c55fb64ecb8c4d7ca3300f7cf3.r2.dev. It contains an OpenStreetMap-derived vector basemap, its label fonts and its style file, and nothing else. A request identifies the map area being drawn. It carries no account, no device identifier and no part of your track — there is no login and no key. As with any web server, Cloudflare records ordinary request logs (IP address, time, the file asked for) for delivery and abuse protection. We do not join those logs to any person, we build no profiles from them, and we do not use them for advertising. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

5. Permissions, and what happens if you refuse

Every permission the app asks for is refusable, and the app is designed to keep working when you refuse.

PermissionIf you say no
Location — While Using the App Recording cannot draw a track. This is the one permission the app genuinely needs, and it is asked for the first time you start recording, not at launch.
Location — Always Nothing breaks. "Always" only lets iOS hand the recording back to the app if the system shuts it down mid-ride. It is insurance, not a requirement, and the app only offers to ask for it after you have completed a recording.
Precise Location The app still records, and labels the track as approximate. It does not nag.
Motion & Fitness Barometric altitude is unavailable, so climb is computed from GPS altitude instead, and stop detection takes slightly longer to be confident. Recording is unaffected.
CameraYou cannot take photos or video inside the app. Everything else works.
MicrophoneYou cannot record voice notes. Everything else works.
Photo LibraryYou cannot add library photos to a ride or save media out to the library.

Background location. Roam declares the background location capability and uses it for one purpose: continuing to record the track you started while the screen is off, the app is in the background, or you are using another app. That is what a route recorder is. Recording only runs in the background while a recording is in progress; when you stop, it stops.

6. Children

Roam is not directed at children and collects nothing from anybody. It contains no advertising, no chat and no user-to-user content. Roam Pro is a single one-time purchase handled entirely by the App Store; buying it collects nothing about you that this policy does not already describe.

7. Your control over your data

Because everything is on your iPhone, you do not have to ask us for anything:

  • Export — any ride, or your entire library, as GPX, KML or CSV, from the ride's share panel or from Settings. The files are yours.
  • Delete a ride — its media and points go with it.
  • Erase everything — Settings has one action that removes every ride, point and media file from the app.
  • Delete the app — iOS removes the app's entire storage with it. Nothing of yours survives anywhere else, because nothing of yours was anywhere else.

There is no account to delete, no data-access request to file and no data-deletion request to send, because we hold nothing about you. If you would like this stated to you in writing anyway, write to the address in section 10 and we will confirm it.
