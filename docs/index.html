In one paragraph

TrailCam has no account and no sign-in. Your trips, and the photos, videos and voice clips attached to them, are stored in a database and in files on your own iPhone, and are never uploaded to us — we operate no server that receives them. Four things do leave the phone, and the Privacy Policy lists all four: crash and error diagnostics, the App Store purchase exchange for the optional Pro upgrade, the map imagery drawn under your track, and — when you finish a trip — the starting coordinate of that trip, sent to a mapping and a weather service to fill in a place name and the weather on the trip card.

TrailCam 没有账号，也没有登录。你的行程，以及附在行程上的照片、视频和语音，都保存在你自己这台 iPhone 的数据库和文件里，绝不会上传给我们 —— 我们没有任何一台服务器会收到它们。确实有四样东西会离开手机， 隐私政策把这四样全部列了出来：崩溃与错误诊断、可选的 Pro 升级所需的 App Store 交易、轨迹底下的地图图像， 以及在你结束一次行程时，把这次行程的起点坐标发给一个地理编码服务和一个天气服务，用来填上行程卡片上的地名和天气。
