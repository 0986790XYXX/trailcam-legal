In one paragraph

Roam is free to download and free to record with. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no cloud sync. Your tracks and the photos, videos and voice notes attached to them are stored on your own iPhone and are never uploaded to us. The app reaches the network only to draw map imagery.
