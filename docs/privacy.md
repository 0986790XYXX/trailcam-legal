# Privacy Policy

**App Name**: TrailCam  
**Developer**: Bingyan Dou  
**Contact**: dcjwf@qq.com  
**Effective Date**: 2026-06-09

## 1. Information We Collect

### Provided by You
- **Account**: Email address for authentication (password stored as encrypted hash)
- **Activity data**: GPS routes, distance, pace, heart rate, elevation, media clips you record

### Collected Automatically
- **Crash reports**: Device model, OS version, stack traces (via Sentry) for bug fixing
- **Anonymous usage stats**: Feature usage patterns without personally identifiable information

### We Do NOT Collect
- Name, phone number, ID documents
- Contacts, photos unrelated to in-app recording
- Persistent location tracking outside active recording sessions

## 2. How We Use Your Data
- Provide route tracking, statistics, and activity history
- Sync your data across devices when signed in
- Diagnose crashes and improve app quality
- We do **not** sell your data to third parties
- We do **not** use your data for advertising

## 3. Third-Party Services

| Service | Purpose | Privacy Policy |
|---------|---------|---------------|
| Supabase | Authentication & data storage | https://supabase.com/privacy |
| Sentry | Crash monitoring | https://sentry.io/privacy/ |
| RevenueCat | Subscription management | https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy |

## 4. Data Security
- All data transmitted over HTTPS/TLS encryption
- Passwords stored using bcrypt hashing — we cannot see your password
- Data stored on Supabase (US/EU servers)

## 5. Your Rights
- **Export**: Download all your data anytime (Settings → Export Data)
- **Delete**: Request account deletion by emailing dcjwf@qq.com
- We will process deletion requests within 30 business days

## 6. Data Retention
Your data is retained as long as your account exists. Upon account deletion, all personal data is permanently removed within 30 days.

## 7. Children
TrailCam is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

## 8. Policy Changes
We will notify you of material changes through the app. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.
